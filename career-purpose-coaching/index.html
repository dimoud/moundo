Purpose & career direction coaching

In short

Career and purpose coaching is for people at a crossroads: a job that no longer fits, a move, a new field or a new chapter. With Alex Moudiotis you clarify what matters now, test options in the real world and turn a vague wish into a plan, in London or online.

Book a discovery call Updated 17 October 2026
01

Does this sound familiar?

Most career questions are really questions about who you are becoming.

  • Your work no longer fits

    It once made sense. Now it feels like someone else’s life.

  • Too many options, or none

    You either cannot choose, or cannot see anything worth choosing.

  • A transition you did not plan

    Redundancy, a move abroad, a break-up or a loss has reset the map.

  • You keep postponing

    You know you want something different, and next year keeps becoming next year.

  • Success that feels empty

    From the outside it works. From the inside, something is missing.

02

How we work on it
  1. 01

    Land

    We talk about where you are, what is pulling you and what is holding you back.

  2. 02

    Map

    We clarify your values, strengths and the stories you tell yourself about what is possible.

  3. 03

    Move

    Small, real-world tests: conversations, short projects, applications, so decisions rest on experience.

  4. 04

    Hold

    We turn the direction into a plan with steps and support that keep you moving.

03

What people usually work towards
  • A direction that fits who you are now
  • Decisions based on real experiments, not endless thinking
  • Confidence to talk about your change and ask for help
  • First concrete steps taken, not just planned
