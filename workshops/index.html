Workshops & training

Workshops for teams and groups

In short

The Inward Lab workshops are interactive sessions for teams, organisations and groups. Led by Alex Moudiotis, who brings a background in teaching and the arts, they combine reflection, creative exercises and practical tools so that insight carries into everyday work and life. Workshops run in London or online.

Enquire about a workshop Updated 17 October 2026
01

What a workshop includes

  • Reflection

    Structured moments to notice patterns: how we react, decide and relate to each other.

  • Creativity

    Exercises drawn from Alex’s background in the visual and applied arts, to see familiar problems from a new angle.

  • Practical tools

    Simple, repeatable practices people can use the next morning, not just remember.

02

Possible themes

Teams who want a session on wellbeing that goes beyond a talk, community groups, and organisations planning a day around growth and change. Every workshop is shaped around your group, so the first step is a conversation.

  • Boundaries and workload: saying no well
  • Confidence and finding your voice
  • Navigating change and transitions
  • Relationships and communication in teams
