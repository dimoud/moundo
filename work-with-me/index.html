Work with me

Three ways to work together

In short

Everything starts with a discovery call: a short conversation to see whether coaching with Alex Moudiotis fits what you need. From there you can continue with ongoing one-to-one coaching, in London or online, or bring a workshop to your team or group.

Book a discovery call Updated 17 October 2026
  • Start here

    Discovery call

    A short, honest conversation about where you are and what you would like to change.

    You get a feel for how Alex works and ask anything you want. There is no obligation to continue.

    Request a discovery call
  • The core work

    1:1 coaching

    Ongoing one-to-one sessions that follow the Inward Lab method: land, map, move, hold.

    Each session ends with something concrete to try in real life, so insight turns into change. Sessions take place in London or online.

    Ask about 1:1 coaching
  • Groups & teams

    Workshops & training

    Interactive sessions that combine reflection, creativity and practical tools, for teams, organisations and groups.

    Themes follow the same four areas: relationships, boundaries, direction and confidence.

    See workshops

In London or online

Alex is based in London and works with clients in person and online. Many people choose online sessions for flexibility; others prefer to meet. You decide what helps you show up most honestly.
