Alex Moudiotis

In short

Alex Moudiotis is a London-based life coach and the founder of The Inward Lab. He is an accredited Elite Life Coach (Level 5, IAPC&M), trained at Evolve Life Coaching College, and brings a background in teaching and the arts. He works with people after break-ups, career changes and life transitions, in London and online.

Book a discovery call Updated 17 October 2026
01

Why I do this work

I came to coaching through teaching and the arts. I studied Fine Arts at the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki and hold an Integrated Master in Visual and Applied Arts. Creativity and connection still shape how I work.

Starting over in a new country taught me what it takes to speak your own truth: to stop editing yourself to fit, and to build a life that reflects who you are. That experience sits at the centre of my coaching.

I founded The Inward Lab because I kept meeting people who understood their problems well but were still stuck inside them. Understanding is not the same as change. My work lives in the gap between the two.

02

How I work

My approach is intuitive and reflective, but also structured. Every insight is tied to an action, so that change happens in your life, not only in the session.
