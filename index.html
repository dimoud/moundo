The Inward Lab method

Coaching with a method, not just a mood.

“Lab” isn’t decoration. The work follows a deliberate arc — so progress feels structured and real, never vague or open-ended.

  1. 01
    Week 0 · free

    Land

    A relaxed discovery call. We name what’s really going on and whether this work is right for you — no pressure, no pitch.

  2. 02
    Sessions 1–2

    Map

    We map the pattern beneath the problem: where it began, what it protects, and what it quietly costs you now.

  3. 03
    The core work

    Move

    Small, deliberate experiments between sessions. This is where insight stops being a thought and becomes a different way of living.

  4. 04
    Integration

    Hold

    We build the structures and self-trust that keep the change in place — long after our sessions end.
