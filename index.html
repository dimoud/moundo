The Inward Lab method

Insight is the start. The work is what you do with it.
  1. 01

    Land

    A first conversation. We name what is really going on and whether this work fits you.

  2. 02

    Map

    We map the pattern under the problem: where it began, what it protects, what it costs you now.

  3. 03

    Move

    Small, deliberate experiments between sessions. Insight becomes a different way of living.

  4. 04

    Hold

    We build the structure and self-trust that keep the change in place after our sessions end.

Meet Alex

A coach shaped by teaching, the arts and starting over.

I’m Alex Moudiotis, a London-based coach with a background in teaching and the arts. I studied Fine Arts at the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki and hold an Integrated Master in Visual and Applied Arts.

Starting over in a new country taught me what it takes to speak your own truth. That is the work I do with clients: often after a break-up, a career change or a life transition.
